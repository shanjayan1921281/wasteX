# Industrial Thermoplastics Recovery & Resin Compounding Protocol (WasteXchange KB-PL-02)
**Authoritative Reference**: WasteXchange Polymer Circularity Standard & Technical Scrap Specification
**Document Code**: `KB-PL-02`
**Category**: `Plastic`
**Keywords**: HDPE, LDPE, Polypropylene, PP purgings, Melt Flow Index (MFI), twin-screw compounding, optical sorting

## 1. Resin Classification & Melt Characteristics
Industrial plastic scrap consists predominantly of post-industrial runners, blow-molding flash, defective extrusions, and start-up purgings:
- **High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE)**: Typical density 0.941-0.965 g/cm³. Injection moulding grade (MFI 4.0-8.0 g/10min) vs Blow moulding grade (MFI 0.2-0.8 g/10min). Cannot be co-processed together without modifying shear viscosity.
- **Polypropylene (PP Copolymer & Homopolymer)**: Density 0.895-0.920 g/cm³. High flexural modulus, susceptible to photo-oxidation if stored uncovered outdoors.
- **Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE / LLDPE Film)**: Post-industrial pallet wrap, blown film trimming. High clarity, tensile elongation > 300%.
- **Multi-layer Laminates (BOPP/PE, PET/PE)**: Complex metallized barrier films. Incompatible for conventional single-screw pelletizing without compatibilizers.

## 2. Technical Quality Thresholds & Permissible Tolerances
- **Cross-Polymer Immiscibility Rule**:
  - Polypropylene in an HDPE stream must NOT exceed 1.5% by weight. Immiscible polyolefin phase boundaries cause severe structural embrittlement and catastrophic failure in molded crates or pressure conduits.
- **Moisture Threshold**:
  - Pellets prior to re-extrusion must have moisture < 0.1% (hydroscopic engineering polymers like PA/PET require < 0.02% via desiccant drying).
- **Melt Flow Index (MFI) Retention**:
  - Secondary reprocessed pellets should retain ≥ 80% of virgin resin MFI. High thermal degradation drops intrinsic viscosity and tensile elongation.
- **Foreign Contamination Limits**:
  - Sand, paper labels, adhesives: < 0.2% mass fraction.
  - PVC contamination: Strictly 0.0%. Even 50ppm PVC generates hydrochloric acid (HCl) during polyolefin extrusion, corroding extruder barrels and degrading polymer chains.

## 3. Circular Recovery & Industrial Pathways
1. **Precision Compounding & Regranulation (Recycling - Primary)**:
   - *Process*: High-torque shredding, multi-stage water wash with caustic surfactant bath (to remove process oils), centrifugal drying, continuous vacuum-degassed twin-screw extruder with 100-mesh dual-screen hydraulic changer.
   - *End Products*: Secondary compounding granules for automotive battery casings, transport pallets, irrigation drip tubing, corrugated conduits.
2. **Structural Thermoplastic Lumber (Recovery - Secondary)**:
   - *Process*: Intrusive extrusion of commingled polyolefins without fine filtration into heavy profile molds (water-cooled).
   - *End Products*: Marine pilings, industrial boardwalk planks, heavy-duty warehouse staging dunnage.
3. **Advanced Pyrolysis Chemical Recycling (Upcycling - Specialty)**:
   - *Process*: Anaerobic thermal cracking at 450°C-550°C in the presence of zeolite catalyst.
   - *End Products*: Pyrolysis oil (naphtha fraction) fed back to petrochemical crackers for circular virgin monomer synthesis.
4. **Co-processing in Cement Kilns (RDF Recovery - Last Resort)**:
   - *Process*: Caloric valorization in pre-calciner cement kilns at 1450°C with total ash incorporation. Applicable only for non-recyclable complex laminated films.

## 4. Market Demand Reality & Commercial Boundaries
- **Market Liquidity**: Clean, natural/transparent post-industrial HDPE and PP regrind trades with near-instant liquidity at 65-80% of virgin virgin polymer index prices.
- **Non-Recyclable Alert**: Thin multi-material laminated packaging with aluminum vapor deposition has ZERO mechanical recycling viability. Do not list as high-grade recycling without compatibilization contracts.
