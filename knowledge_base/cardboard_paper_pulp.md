# Corrugated Container Scrap (OCC) & Paper Pulp Circularity Spec (WasteXchange KB-CB-05)
**Authoritative Reference**: WasteXchange Secondary Fiber Technical Specification & ISRI Grade Standards
**Document Code**: `KB-CB-05`
**Category**: `Cardboard`
**Keywords**: Old Corrugated Containers (OCC), Kraft paperboard, hydropulping, burst factor, stickies, de-inking

## 1. Material Classification & Fiber Integrity
Cellulose fiber degrades physically with each recycling cycle due to hornification (irreversible pore collapse inside fiber cell walls):
- **Grade 11 OCC (Industrial Corrugated Scrap)**: Corrugated packaging generated in manufacturing plants. Contains double-wall and single-wall boxes with virgin kraft liners (Burst Factor > 22).
- **Grade 12 Double Sorted Corrugated (DS OCC)**: Specially selected corrugated containers from supermarket and manufacturing streams, completely free of foreign boxboard or waxed containers.
- **Kraft Multiwall Bag Cuttings**: Unprinted unbleached virgin sulfate kraft paper offcuts from cement and chemical sack converting plants.

## 2. Technical Quality Thresholds & Permissible Tolerances
- **Moisture Threshold**: Equilibrium moisture must not exceed 10.0-12.0%. Moisture > 14.0% leads to automatic weight deduction penalties (due to artificial water weight), mold spore cultivation, and microbiological fiber breakdown.
- **Prohibitive Materials (Zero Tolerance)**:
  - Wax-impregnated or hot-melt coated boxes (produce microscopic sticky globules called "stickies" that foul paper machine forming wires and dry felt).
  - Bitumen-coated waterproof wrappers, foil laminates, wet-strength resin cartons.
- **Total Outthrows**: Must not exceed 1.0% (inclusive of plastic strapping bands, shipping pouches, thermal tape).

## 3. Circular Recovery & Industrial Pathways
1. **Hydropulping & Recycled Packaging Board (Recycling - Primary)**:
   - *Process*: Batch or continuous high-consistency hydropulping (12-16% consistency), continuous ragger wire for bale wire and tape removal, pressurized perforated basket screening, centricleaning to separate high-density grit, paper machine multi-ply forming.
   - *End Products*: Recycled fluting medium (corrugating medium) and testliner linerboard (120-250 gsm).
2. **Molded Pulp Protective Packaging (Upcycling - Specialty)**:
   - *Process*: Refining secondary kraft pulp, blending with hydrophobic alkyl ketene dimer (AKD) sizing agent, vacuum thermoforming into 3D protective packaging clamshells.
   - *End Products*: Eco-friendly electronics buffers, cosmetic trays, biodegradable protective end-caps.
3. **Cellulose Hydroseeding & Agricultural Mulch (Recovery - Secondary)**:
   - *Process*: Shredding with organic green colorant and fertilizer for erosion control spray slurries along highway embankments.

## 4. Market Demand Reality & Regional Sourcing
- **Market Demand**: Permanent, highly predictable demand across every industrial manufacturing hub. Corrugator packaging mills depend on steady OCC inflows for up to 85% of their fiber furnish.
- **Pricing Sensitivity**: Heavy price discounts applied if bales show outdoor weathering, excessive soot, or moisture saturation.
