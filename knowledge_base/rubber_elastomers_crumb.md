# Elastomer Byproducts, Curing Scrap & Devulcanization Engineering (WasteXchange KB-RB-04)
**Authoritative Reference**: WasteXchange Elastomer Recycling Protocol & Devulcanization Standard
**Document Code**: `KB-RB-04`
**Category**: `Rubber`
**Keywords**: Vulcanized rubber, EPDM, SBR, devulcanization, crumb rubber, crumb modified bitumen (CRMB)

## 1. Material Classification & Polymer Crosslinking
Industrial elastomer waste is fundamentally distinct from thermoplastics because irreversible covalent sulfur cross-links prevent conventional remelting:
- **Uncured Compound Scrap**: Green rubber tire tread offcuts and calendar friction waste. Retains virgin polymer chain mobility and can be direct-blended back into masterbatch Banbury mixers (up to 15% ratio).
- **Vulcanized EPDM Scrap**: Extruded weatherstrips, automotive radiator hoses, roofing membrane punchings. High heat and ozone resistance.
- **Crumb Buffings (SBR / Natural Rubber)**: Tyre retreading buffings, belt manufacturing shavings. High tensile strength, free of structural bead wire.

## 2. Technical Quality Thresholds & Permissible Tolerances
- **Moisture Threshold**: < 1.0% for crumb rubber intended for modified bitumen or compounding.
- **Foreign Inclusion Limits**:
  - Ferrous wire content: Strictly < 0.01% by weight (extracted using multi-stage magnetic pulleys).
  - Cord and textile fluff: < 0.2% mass fraction (separated via zig-zag air classifiers).
  - Acetone extractables: Typical range 8-14% (indicating oil and additive loading).
- **Particle Size Uniformity**:
  - 10-20 mesh (0.85-2.0mm): Outdoor playground safety surfaces, athletic tracks.
  - 30-40 mesh (0.42-0.60mm): Highway asphalt modification (CRMB 55 / 60 specifications).
  - 60-80 mesh (0.18-0.25mm): Acoustic dampening composites and molded automotive seals.

## 3. Circular Recovery & Industrial Pathways
1. **Thermomechanical & Chemical Devulcanization (Recycling - Primary)**:
   - *Process*: Twin-screw continuous devulcanization reactor applying high shear and temperature (180°C-220°C) with green alkyl disulfide devulcanizing agents that selectively cleave sulfur-sulfur bonds (S-S) without breaking backbone carbon-carbon chains.
   - *End Products*: Reclaim rubber slabs with 50-70% tensile retention, substituted into conveyor belt core and solid tire inner compounds.
2. **Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen - CRMB (Recovery - Secondary)**:
   - *Process*: High-shear dispersion of 30-mesh rubber crumb into virgin bitumen (VG-30/VG-40) at 190°C for 45 minutes, creating a cross-linked viscoelastic binder.
   - *End Products*: High-durability asphalt roads with superior resistance to rutting, fatigue cracking, and water stripping.
3. **Molded Rubber Tiles & Acoustic Insulation (Direct Reuse / Upcycling)**:
   - *Process*: Ambient blending with aromatic MDI polyurethane binder (8-12%) followed by hydraulic compression curing at 140°C.

## 4. Market Demand Reality & Technical Misconception
- **Critical Science Rule**: Never declare vulcanized rubber scrap as "melt-recyclable". Any buyer attempting to melt cured rubber without devulcanization will decompose the polymer into hazardous pyrolysis gasses and charred carbon.
- **Market Demand**: Steady high demand for 30-40 mesh clean crumb rubber driven by state highway department infrastructure mandates and civil engineering projects.
