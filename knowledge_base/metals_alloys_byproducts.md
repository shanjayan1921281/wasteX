# Metallurgical Scrap Classification & Foundry Charging Specifications (WasteXchange KB-MT-03)
**Authoritative Reference**: WasteXchange Metallurgical Secondary Raw Materials Benchmark & ASTM Charging Code
**Document Code**: `KB-MT-03`
**Category**: `Metal`
**Keywords**: Ferrous turnings, CNC swarf, aluminum dross, copper wire scrap, briquetting, induction melting

## 1. Material Classification & Alloy Chemistry
Metal scrap from machining, stamping, die-casting, and extrusion processes is grouped by base element and physical bulk density:
- **Ferrous Turning & Boring Swarf**: Spiral turnings and fine chips from lathe, CNC, and broaching operations. Low bulk density (0.4-0.8 t/m³) requiring mechanical compaction.
- **Aluminum Extrusion Scrap & Turnings (6000 Series / 6063-T6)**: Clean profile punchings and clean unpainted turnings with known Mg-Si stoichiometry.
- **Aluminum Skimmings & White Dross**: Oxidic metallic mixture skimming from melting crucibles containing 40-75% free aluminum metal trapped in an alumina/salt matrix.
- **Copper & Brass Heavy Scrap (IS 319 / ASTM B36)**: Millberry copper wire (minimum 99.9% Cu), honey brass turnings, stamping skeletons.

## 2. Technical Quality Thresholds & Permissible Tolerances
- **Hydrocarbon & Cutting Fluid Threshold**:
  - Unwashed machining chips frequently carry 4-12% moisture and emulsified water-soluble cutting oils.
  - Oil content prior to melting must be reduced to < 2.0% via centrifugal wringer or thermal de-oiling; charging oily chips into open induction furnaces causes explosive vapor flash, heavy black smoke, and high slag metal entrapping.
- **Tramp Iron (Ferrous Contamination in Non-Ferrous)**:
  - Aluminum or brass scrap must have < 0.1% tramp iron. Double inline neodymium overband magnetic separators are mandatory.
- **Oxidation & Loss on Ignition (LOI)**:
  - Loose fine chips suffer up to 18-25% oxidation burn-off loss during melting if not briquetted into high-density pucks (>2.1 g/cm³ for aluminum, >5.5 g/cm³ for cast iron).

## 3. Circular Recovery & Industrial Pathways
1. **High-Pressure Hydraulic Briquetting & Induction Melting (Recycling - Primary)**:
   - *Process*: Centrifugal spin-drying of coolant, magnetic purification, 350-bar cold hydraulic briquetting, charging into coreless medium-frequency induction furnaces with Argon purging.
   - *End Products*: Secondary alloy billets, automotive brake disc castings, aluminum sand-casting ingots (ADC12, LM6).
2. **Rotary Salt Furnace Dross Recovery (Recovery - Secondary)**:
   - *Process*: Tilting rotary furnace melting with potassium chloride/sodium chloride flux to coalesce microscopic aluminum droplets away from oxidic skin.
   - *End Products*: De-oxidant cones for steelmaking, secondary casting ingots.
   - *Residue Management*: Generates non-metallic salt cake requiring TSDF approved handling.
3. **Powder Metallurgy & Sintered Components (Upcycling - Specialty)**:
   - *Process*: Annealing, pulverization, and grading of pure copper/iron swarf into 100-300 mesh powder for press-and-sinter bearing bushes.

## 4. Market Demand Reality & Price Liquidity
- **Exceptional Liquidity**: Industrial metals command immediate cash settlements benchmarked against London Metal Exchange (LME) and domestic spot indexes.
- **Critical Surcharge Notice**: Scrap contaminated with leaded brass chips mixed into non-ferrous aluminum is severely discounted (up to 50%) or rejected due to alloy contamination risk.
